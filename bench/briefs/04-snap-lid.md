with a small overhang, plus a rim or tabs that locate inside the box walls, plus at least
two snap hooks that catch under the box's top edge. Choose sensible hook and clearance
dimensions for FDM printing and state them in your script. One solid, Z up, lid top facing
up.

open, so any band would be invented; the volume check reports `SKIP`. This brief is mainly
for the human review: do the hooks look like they would print and catch?

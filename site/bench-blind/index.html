Benchmark pilot: blind labelling

Each ID is one run, in random order, with the brief the agent was given and its four views. Tool names, scripts and check results are hidden. Record accept (yes, no or unsure) and the rubric's reason codes (bench/rubric.md) in bench/labels-blind.csv; use unsure when the views can't settle it. Curves drawn as visible facets can hint at the tool; judge the part, not the mesh.

B01

A tray that holds a standard 96-tip pipette rack whose footprint is 122 × 80 mm. One solid, Z up: - a flat base 3 mm thick; - a perimeter wall 2 mm thick and 10 mm tall, standing on the base; - 0.5 mm clearance between the rack and the inside of the wall on every side, so the inside of the wall is 123 × 81 mm and the outside of the part is 127 × 85 mm. The part is 13 mm tall overall; the inside is open from the top.

B02

A snap-fit lid for an open-topped box whose outer footprint is 50 × 30 mm and whose walls are 1.5 mm thick, to be 3D-printed in PETG. The lid is a flat top plate covering the box with a small overhang, plus a rim or tabs that locate inside the box walls, plus at least two snap hooks that catch under the box's top edge. Choose sensible hook and clearance dimensions for FDM printing and state them in your script. One solid, Z up, lid top facing up.

B03

An L-bracket, one solid, Z up. Base: a plate 40 mm (X) × 30 mm (Y) × 5 mm thick, lying flat. Upright: a wall 40 mm (X) wide and 5 mm thick (Y), standing on the base along one 40 mm edge, so the bracket is 30 mm tall overall. Two through-holes, 5 mm diameter, go vertically through the base only, 24 mm apart along X and centred in X, located 5 mm from the base's centre line towards the open edge (away from the upright).
